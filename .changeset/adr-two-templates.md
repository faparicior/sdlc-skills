`adr-create` now has two ADR templates: `TEMPLATE-STRUCTURAL.md` (structure and technology decisions whose rules feed architecture tests, with an Enforceable Rules table) and `TEMPLATE-PROBLEM-DRIVEN.md` (decisions that answer an observed problem, with Measurements, a Mermaid flow and a Design table). Both add a Summary table, a risk table and a comparison matrix. The skill asks for the type before writing, and `adr-validate` checks the new sections.
